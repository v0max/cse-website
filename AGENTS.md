## Content

All site content is data in `content/` (see MAINTAINING.md); pages are generated
from it by `src/lib/content.ts`. Add or change people, cases and site text there,
not in components. Portraits in `public/portraits/` are generated by
`tools/portraits/build.py`; never edit them by hand.
