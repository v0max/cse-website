Stuart Schechter researches computer security and privacy, human behavior and ethics.

His research helped debunk mandatory 90-day password changes, password-complexity policies, security questions and site-authentication images.

In privacy investigations, he builds software that tracks the flow of information from websites to third parties, and that analyzes exported analytics data and configuration files such as Google Tag Manager containers.

He is an Associate of the Harvard School of Engineering and Applied Sciences. He previously lectured at UC Berkeley, was a researcher at Microsoft Research from 2007 to 2016, and was a member of the technical staff at MIT Lincoln Laboratory from 2004 to 2007.
