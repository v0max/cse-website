Bruce Schneier is an internationally renowned security technologist; The Economist has called him a security guru.

His work spans cryptography, cybersecurity, digital privacy and data integrity, AI security and safety, IoT security, corporate and government surveillance, digital fraud, blockchains, and cybersecurity policy, including how AI intersects with democracy and governance.

He is a Lecturer in Public Policy at the Harvard Kennedy School and at the University of Toronto’s Munk School, a fellow at Harvard’s Berkman Klein Center for Internet & Society, and Chief of Security Architecture at Inrupt. He serves on the boards of the Electronic Frontier Foundation and AccessNow and on the advisory boards of EPIC and VerifiedVoting.org.

He is the New York Times best-selling author of 14 books, including *A Hacker’s Mind* and *Rewiring Democracy*, and hundreds of articles, essays and academic papers. His newsletter *Crypto-Gram* and blog *Schneier on Security* are read by more than 250,000 people. He has testified before Congress more than half a dozen times and has served as an expert witness in class-action privacy suits, contract disputes and patent disputes.
