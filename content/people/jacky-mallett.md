Jacky Mallett studies the behavior and constraints of complex networked systems, in computing and in economics.

She is an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at Reykjavik University, where she directs the Frostbyte Cybersecurity Research group, focused on practical cyber defence for small countries. She teaches Cyber Defence, Computer Networks, Industrial Control Security, and Financial Analysis and Simulation.

Her work on banking identified several feedback loops in banking regulation, including the asset-backed security loophole linked to the 2008 financial crisis. She wrote Threadneedle, a simulation of fractional reserve banking built on double-entry bookkeeping.

She previously developed consumer networking solutions at Sony Research in Belgium, worked as a developer at the high-frequency trading firm Madison Tyler, and was a manager in Nortel Networks’ X.25 and Frame Relay division. She served on the Supervisory Board of the Central Bank of Iceland, appointed by the Icelandic Parliament.
