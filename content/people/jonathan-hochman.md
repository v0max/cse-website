Jonathan Hochman is a computer scientist and seasoned consulting and testifying expert in software, internet technologies and digital marketing.

His expertise covers software engineering, internet technologies, cybersecurity, digital identity, data privacy, e-commerce, digital advertising, search engine optimization and pay-per-click advertising. He holds a PhD in computer science from Yale, where he has taught courses in cybersecurity, digital identity, privacy and secure software development.

Since 2007 he has been retained by plaintiffs and defendants in hundreds of matters involving patents, trademarks, copyrights, trade secrets, contracts, defamation and online reputation, unfair competition and fraud. He has testified at 24 trials and 76 depositions in U.S. federal and state courts, in arbitrations, in the Federal Court of Canada and in the District Court of Israel.

He is a named inventor on three U.S. patents and co-founded CodeGuard, a cybersecurity company acquired by Sectigo.
