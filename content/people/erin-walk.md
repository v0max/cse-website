Erin Walk is a computational social scientist who studies digital communication, social media and online behavior.

Her research examines how people interact with digital technology, how information spreads, and how platform design shapes communication and public discourse. She works with large-scale platform data, survey research, experiments and computational methods.

She previously worked in technology policy at Cloudflare and researched political communication and digital information ecosystems at the University of Pennsylvania.
