Sara Lazarus is a security practitioner whose experience spans incident response, security operations, and designing and assessing enterprise security programs.

Her hands-on work includes breach investigation and response, security operations center leadership, corporate IT security, and compliance with SOC 2, ISO 27001, NIST 800-53 and CMMC.

She has led incident response and security operations teams, built the 24×7 Security Operations Center and incident response team for Salesforce’s GovCloud environments, and served as the first Head of Security and IT at a high-growth fintech, where she built security, DevOps and corporate IT from the ground up. She is the founder of Faded Jeans Technology LLC.
