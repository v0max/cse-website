Serge Egelman studies how people make privacy and security decisions, and how mobile devices and apps handle the personal data entrusted to them.

He is a research scientist in EECS at UC Berkeley, where he directs the Berkeley Laboratory for Usable and Experimental Security (BLUES). He co-founded AppCensus, which analyzes the privacy behavior of mobile apps at scale, and serves on the advisory board of EPIC.

His research has led to congressional testimony, lawsuits and regulatory actions, and has been covered by the New York Times, Washington Post, Wall Street Journal, Wired, CNET, NBC and CBS.
