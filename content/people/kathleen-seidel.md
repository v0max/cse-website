Kathleen Seidel provides research services to Computer Science Experts.

She has worked as a children’s librarian and a website developer, and is the proprietor of Neurodiversity.com.

She is co-author, with Bruce Schneier, of *A Worldwide Survey of Encryption Products* (2016), and, with historian David Perry, of the Ruderman White Paper on media coverage of the murder of people with disabilities by their caregivers (2017).
