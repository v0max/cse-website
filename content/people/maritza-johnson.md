Maritza Johnson studies human-centered security and privacy, and why online privacy controls so often fail the people who rely on them.

Her research covers usable privacy settings, social network privacy, smartphone permissions and privacy expectations for connected home devices. She is the founding director of the Center for Digital Civil Society at the University of San Diego, where she also created the AI Ethics course for the M.S. in Applied Artificial Intelligence program.

She has worked as a user experience researcher at Google and a technical privacy manager at Facebook, as a researcher at the International Computer Science Institute, and as a lecturer at the UC Berkeley School of Information.

She contributed to *Toward Trustworthy AI Development: Mechanisms for Supporting Verifiable Claims* and has served on the National Academies Forum on Cyber Resilience.
